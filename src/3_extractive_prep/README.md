# Step 3: extractive-industry payments (resource correction inputs)

Multinationals' country-by-country reports include the profit and the taxes of oil, gas and mining operations. These are taxed under their own regimes (royalties, production sharing, resource taxes), and unitary taxation of corporate profit would not move them. Script 4 therefore takes the profit and tax that come from extraction out of the reports before the estimation. This folder builds the panel of resource payments that script 4 uses: for each headquarters country, source country, commodity and year, the payments the multinationals of that headquarters country make to the source country's government.

The scripts are numbered in run order, each stage moving to a finer level:

| Stage | Scripts | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| 3_1x | `3_11`–`3_17` | Read and clean each source: EITI government revenues, production volumes and payment splits, the UNU-WIDER Government Revenue Dataset, World Bank resource rents, EIA energy production, BGS mineral production |
| 3_2x | `3_21`–`3_26` | Combine them into a country × year panel of government resource revenue and rents (calibrated rent fractions, combined rents, the consolidated royalty panel, classification fixes) |
| 3_3x | `3_31`–`3_36` | Bring the payments to headquarters × source country × year: EITI company payments, matched to their headquarters country through Orbis; operators' payments-to-governments reports; the EITI bilateral panel; Orbis headquarters shares of extractive revenue |
| 3_38 | `3_38_resource_payments_by_hq_source.py` | The final cascade: documented payments first (EITI company payments, operator reports, the company tables in `data/raw/extractive/source_tables/`, hand-curated national totals and foreign shares); the part of a national total's foreign share that no document or curated share covers is assigned to no headquarters country |

Shared modules: `_paths.py` (folders), `_reference_prices.py` (commodity reference prices), `_resource_scope.py` (which payers count as extractive).

**What runs in the replication.** The replication starts at 3_38. Step 3_38 needs the record-level match of EITI companies to Orbis companies (`eiti_company_hq_map.csv`), which may not be redistributed, so `src/_run_pipeline.py` runs it only where that file is present. Otherwise script 4 reads the two outputs of 3_38 shipped in `data/intermediate/extractive/` (`resource_payments_by_hq_source_yearly.csv` and `company_anchored_strips.csv`, aggregated to headquarters country × source country × year). The package also ships the outputs of 3_38 for the sensitivity without the inferred company-level resource profit (the files ending in `__res_no_inferred_companies`), the only dataset variant that changes an input of 3_38. The other inputs of script 4 from these stages (combined rents, operator payments and the country pairs with documented EITI payments) are shipped too, as are the inputs of 3_38 that do not contain Orbis records. The scripts of 3_1x–3_36 are included so that every step can be followed and rerun with the original inputs, which `data/raw/README.md` ("Not included") lists; the company tables in `data/raw/extractive/source_tables/` are shipped without the rows that came from Orbis. Run the scripts from the repository root, e.g. `python src/3_extractive_prep/3_38_resource_payments_by_hq_source.py`.
